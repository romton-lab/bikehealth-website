Release notes

How BikeHealth has evolved, version after version.

2.5

August 2026 Current version

First public release. Here is everything BikeHealth does today. Later versions will stack on top of this baseline.

Your bikes

  • A catalogue of nearly 400 models across 39 brands, e-bikes included: components are created with their brand and model. Manual entry is available too, with standard thresholds you can refine afterwards.
  • Custom photo from your gallery.
  • Bike details screen: name, badge colour, type, date added, starting mileage, model year, brakes, drivetrain, frame, fork, e-bike, derailleurs, free-form notes.
  • Already-ridden bike: when adding it, you declare which parts are new or recently replaced, so you don't get false alerts on day one.
  • Several bikes, each with its own tracking, alerts and history. Archive a bike that no longer rides.

Components and wear

  • Tracking with alerts: chain or belt, cassette, front and rear tyres, disc brake pads or rim blocks, front and rear derailleurs, chainring, wheels and spokes, and for e-bikes the battery and motor.
  • Gauge and status, green, amber or red, for every part.
  • Thresholds you can check: drawn from manufacturer recommendations and workshop practice, they account for e-bikes and are spelled out in the "Our thresholds" section of your profile.
  • Brand and model editable per component.
  • Replacement history per part: you can see how many kilometres each successive chain lasted.

Trips and mileage

  • Regular trips declared once (Commute, Sport, Leisure, Errands) and counted automatically, with no sensor.
  • Weekly confirmation in one tap, on the day and at the time of your choosing.
  • Manual entry for a one-off trip, with the type editable afterwards.
  • Holiday mode: trip generation pauses for the dates you're away and resumes on its own when you're back.
  • Trip history grouped by month.

Maintenance and alerts

  • Four recurring reminders per bike: chain lubrication (150 km), tyre pressure (200 km or 14 days), brake check (300 km or 30 days), annual service (365 days).
  • Custom reminders created from a bike's page, for what the app doesn't track on its own: insurance, a lock to replace.
  • Snooze a reminder by 3 days or 50 km, straight from the notification.
  • Alerts tab with a permanent badge: you can tell at a glance whether anything needs doing.
  • Log maintenance in two taps from the alert, with notes, cost and a "full service" tick box.
  • Maintenance history grouped by month, filterable by type.
  • Notifications grouped by bike, either immediately or in a fixed slot you choose, and silent from 10pm to 8am in immediate mode.

Tips

  • 15 guides available in the app, across three areas.
  • Something's wrong: I've punctured, my brakes feel soft, the chain has dropped or snapped, the gears won't shift cleanly, something's rubbing or making a noise, something else. Safety rule first, repair second.
  • Taking care: the quick pre-ride check, the weekly routine, lubricating your chain properly, the monthly once-over, your saddlebag kit, looking after your safety gear.
  • Replacing: the chain, the cassette, a tyre or an inner tube.
  • Shop shortcut from every guide: reach your favourite shop, or find one open nearby.

Statistics

  • Riding rhythm.
  • Maintenance cost, worked out from the amounts you log.
  • Environmental impact: CO₂ avoided compared with an average petrol car, on ADEME figures.
  • Split by trip type, filterable by bike.

Profile and settings

  • Guided setup on first launch, replayable from Settings.
  • Favourite shop: search by name or address, pre-filled details (name, address, phone, opening hours), manual entry available, buttons to call it and to get directions.
  • Notification settings broken down by reminder family.
  • Send feedback from inside the app.
  • Data: anonymous statistics sharing can be switched off, and a single button deletes all your data.
