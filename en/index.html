Features

BikeHealth doesn't replace a rider's eye. It gives it a memory.

Made for people who ride every day. No mechanic required.

Component tracking

Chain, cassette, tyres, brake pads, chainring, wheels, battery and motor (e-bike). Every part has its own threshold and gauge: green, amber, red.

Trip routines

Set your regular trips once (commute, errands, Sunday ride) and the app counts them for you. You confirm your week in one tap.

Maintenance reminders

Lubrication, tyre pressure, brakes, annual service: the reminder lands when it's due. Add your own, for insurance or a lock to replace.

Tips

You puncture, your brakes feel soft, something is rubbing: 15 guides written for the roadside. Safety first, the repair second.

Favourite shop

Save your trusted mechanic: their hours, phone number and directions are one tap away from every bike profile, and from the Tips section.

History, part by part

You know how many kilometres your last chain lasted, and whether the next one does better. Every replacement is logged in two taps.

More than one bike? Each gets its own tracking, alerts and history.

How it works

Five steps to ride with peace of mind.

Set up your profile

The day you want to confirm your week, and when you want your notifications to arrive. Three minutes.

Add your bike

Find it in the catalogue and its components are created with their brand and model. Otherwise, enter it by hand with standard thresholds, to fine-tune whenever you like.

Set your routines

The commute, the errands, the Sunday ride. Once and for all.

Ride with peace of mind

You confirm your week in one tap. The app works out the wear and alerts you when a part reaches its threshold. Not before.

Log your maintenance

Two taps from the alert. The counter resets, the history fills up.
