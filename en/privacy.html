Translation notice: this English version is provided for convenience. The French version at bikehealth.fr/confidentialite is the legally authoritative text. In case of discrepancy between the two, the French version prevails.

1. Contact

BikeHealth is an independent project. For any question or request about your data: contact@bikehealth.fr

2. What is stored on your phone

Your bikes, their components, your trips, your maintenance records, your routines, your profile and your favourite shop are saved on your device. A button in Settings deletes the lot. Uninstalling the app erases them too.

3. Shop search

When you search for a shop, whether to save your favourite or to find one that's open from a Tips page, your query is sent to Google (Places and Maps) to return results. For a nearby search, the app asks for your location, only at the moment of the search and only once you agree. No search is triggered without an action from you.

4. Crash reports and usage statistics

The app sends anonymous crash reports and usage measurements, to fix bugs and understand which screens are useful. They cannot identify you and contain nothing about your bikes or your trips. You can switch them off in Settings, under Data.

5. Website

This site uses cookie-free audience measurement with no advertising tracker, which is why there is no consent banner. If you fill in the bike shop form, your email address and answers are used solely to get back to you.
